# Aarchi's by Archana Soni — Brand Book (storefront)

> *Where Tradition Meets Trend.* The studio's own line, kept verbatim.
> Made to measure in Ahmedabad. Shipped worldwide.

This is the identity for the e-commerce storefront. It replaced the v1 *Taana–Baana* concept
(dark "kajal" surfaces, Bodoni, woven motifs, scroll-jacked 3D hero), which is recoverable from
git commit `c246af5`. The brief for v2 was a proper, world-class fashion store, so the photographs
now carry the luxury and the interface steps back.

---

## 1. The idea: the gallery and the atelier

Think of a couture house's flagship store. White walls, good light and garments given room.
The customer never has to wonder how to buy.

- **Photography first.** The studio's own campaign shoots and product photos fill the frame.
  Interface chrome is black, white and hairlines, and nothing competes with the embroidery.
- **Familiar commerce.** The store follows the patterns shoppers already know from the best
  fashion stores: announcement bar, mega-menu, search, filter sidebar, quick view, cart drawer,
  sticky buy box and a Shopify-style checkout. Luxury here means calm and confidence, not novelty.
- **The atelier shows through.** "Made to measure", the WhatsApp stylist and Archana's own process
  appear at every decision point. That is what separates this house from a marketplace.

## 2. Wordmark and icon

- **Wordmark:** `AARCHI’S` in Cormorant Garamond 600, tracked +0.2em, with `BY ARCHANA SONI`
  beneath in Inter 500 at 9px, tracked +0.34em. It sits centred in the header.
  Component: `src/components/brand/Logo.tsx`.
- **Icon / favicon:** a Cormorant "A", white on ink (`public/favicon.svg` is an outline path, so it
  needs no font). The app icons and share image are generated by `scripts/brand_assets.py` from the
  self-hosted fonts and the studio's photos.
- **Don't:** outline the wordmark, set it in another face, put it on a photo without a scrim, or
  place the old swirl logo next to it.

## 4. Type

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond (variable, 300–700). Used for page titles, section titles,
  product names on the product page, and the wordmark. Weight 500, tight leading.
- **Text and UI:** Inter (variable). Body text is 15px. Labels are 11px in tracked capitals,
  and buttons are 12px tracked capitals. Card names are 14px.
- The ₹ glyph comes from a 1.2 KB Inter subset (`inter-rupee.woff2`), so prices never fall back
  to a system font.
- Both fonts are self-hosted under SIL OFL (`public/fonts/LICENSE-*.txt`).

## 5. Voice: warm, precise, never pushy

We sound like Archana at her cutting table. That means short sentences and craft named plainly
(*khat work, zardozi, bandhej*). We say what happens next and never oversell. Commerce labels use
the standard words shoppers scan for: *Add to bag*, *Checkout*, *Wishlist*, *Size guide*.

| Moment | We say |
|---|---|
| Primary purchase action | **Add to bag** → the bag drawer opens |
| Checkout's final action | **Reserve & confirm** (no payment is taken; see DESIGN.md §6) |
| Size selector | **Made to measure** is the default, followed by XS–XXL and any saved measurements |
| Empty bag | **Your bag is empty.** Every piece is cut to your measurements… |
| No results | **Nothing matches “…” yet.** Every design can be made to your brief. |
| Help | **Ask a stylist on WhatsApp** / **Chat with a stylist** |
| 404 | **We couldn’t find that page.** |

Rules:
1. Prices read "From ₹…". Placeholder prices always carry *indicative* and never go into
   structured data. We never say "sale" or "discount".
2. We never promise a timeline, price, policy or size chart the studio hasn't published. We say
   *confirmed with your quote*.
3. Product copy is the studio's own words, shown with its provenance (see DESIGN.md §7).
4. No emoji in the site's voice. Instagram captions shown as quotes keep their words but lose their emoji.

## 6. Imagery

- The studio's own photography only, from the live site and its Instagram campaigns. No stock
  images and no AI-generated people.
- **No baked-in text in curated spots.** Several campaign slides and promo cards have headlines
  printed into the pixels ("ROYAL SCARLET…", "Aarchi's Presents…"). Hero, feature, occasion and
  campaign tiles use only clean frames. Product grids show each product's real photo, whatever it is.
- Product cards are 3:4. Tall or awkward originals are shown whole on an ivory mat
  (`fit: contain`) rather than cropped through the garment.
- Hover reveals the on-figure "look" frame from the piece's own campaign, where one exists.
- The 3D garment study is labelled an interpretive study. The photograph is always the true reference.
