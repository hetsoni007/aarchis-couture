## Copy quality — the honest picture

- **27** products have live copy that actually describes the garment. It is shown as the description.
- **17** products have live copy that is a promotional Instagram caption (“Fall in love with fashion all over again…”). It is kept verbatim and shown as a quoted caption *from the studio's Instagram*, not as a garment description.
- **4** products have live copy that is a broken caption fragment (stripped hashtags/handles, someone else's credits, or another brand's name). It is kept in the data but **not displayed**.
- No product description was invented to fill a gap. Where the copy is thin, the product page leans on the facts we do have (category, occasion and style tags, fabric where named, crafts where named, the photograph).
