The Studio

A label built on craft & care

Founded by Archana Soni in Ahmedabad, Aarchi's crafts timeless designs that celebrate elegance and individuality — from bridal lehengas with zari, zardozi and khat work to handcrafted sarees and festive wear.

Every piece is made to measure and designed around you, blending traditional craftsmanship with a contemporary sensibility.

Our story

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you make custom, made-to-measure outfits?

Yes — every piece at Aarchi's is made to measure. Bridal lehengas, sarees, festive and Navratri wear, baby-shower and maternity outfits are designed around your measurements, and the colour, fabric and embroidery of any catalogue design can be customised.

Do you ship outside India — USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Many of our clients are NRI brides and families ordering custom Indian wedding and festive outfits from abroad. Share your requirement on WhatsApp and we'll guide you through measurements, fittings and delivery.

How do I order a custom bridal lehenga or saree?

Browse the catalogue, pick a design you love (or bring your own reference), and enquire on WhatsApp. Archana will discuss the design, fabric and budget with you, guide your measurements, and craft the piece in her Ahmedabad studio.

Can I change the colour or fabric of a catalogue design?

Absolutely. Every design is a starting point — colours, fabrics, embroidery and silhouettes are tailored to you. Tell us what you'd like to change when you enquire.

Where is the studio located?

The studio is in Ahmedabad, Gujarat — visits by appointment. Outstation and international clients are looked after end-to-end on WhatsApp.
